02 / Origin story

It usually starts
with “why not?”

I was bored one day, looking around at different websites and projects, and started thinking: why not make my own game or some kind of system to kill boredom?

That question became real projects. One of the biggest origins was a controller with stick drift. I wanted to play Rocket League, so I researched how to fix it, fixed it, and then built PulsePad so I could test controllers myself.

I like programming and creating content too. My YouTube is inactive while school and projects take the front seat, but making things is still the part I keep coming back to.
